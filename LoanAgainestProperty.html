Loan Against Property

A Loan Against Property (LAP) allows property owners to unlock the value of their residential or commercial property to meet important financial requirements.

The funds can be used for a variety of legitimate personal or business purposes, including business expansion, working capital, education, medical expenses or other major financial requirements.

By leveraging an existing property, borrowers can access structured financing while continuing to use the property as permitted under the applicable loan terms.

Property You Can Leverage

Residential Property

Eligible residential properties may be considered as collateral, subject to lender-specific requirements.

Commercial Property

Commercial properties may be considered for financing based on property and applicant eligibility.

Owned Property

Use the value of an eligible owned property to meet substantial financial requirements.
