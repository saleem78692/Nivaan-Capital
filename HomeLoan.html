Eligibility Criteria

Check the basic eligibility requirements before applying for your home loan.

Applicant Type

Self-employed professionals, non-professionals and salaried individuals can apply.

Steady Income

Applicant must have a steady and regular income.

Age Criteria

Applicant must be between 21 and 65 years of age.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Documents Required for Home Loan

When applying for a home loan, there are documents you must keep handy. Here is a list of documents required for a home loan, curated for you.

For Salaried

Documents generally required from salaried applicants.

  • Proof of Identity and Address – Aadhaar card/PAN card/Passport/ Voter’s ID/Driving License
  • Proof of Income
  • PAN Card
  • Bank Statements of the Last 12 Months
  • Details of all live loans

For Self-Employed

Documents generally required from self-employed applicants.

  • Proof of Identity and Address – Aadhaar card/PAN card/Passport/ Voter’s ID/Driving License
  • Proof of Income / Business Income
  • PAN Card
  • Bank Statements of the Last 12 Months
  • Details of all live loans
  • Business Registration / Business Proof

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about Nivaan Capital Home Loans, eligibility, documents, EMI, tenure and repayment options.

Self-employed professionals, self-employed non-professionals and salaried individuals can apply for a Nivaan Home Loan, subject to the applicable eligibility criteria. The applicant should have a steady source of income.

Nivaan Home Loans can be used for self-construction of a home, purchase of ready-to-occupy homes, balance transfer of an existing home loan, mixed-use properties and eligible shop loans.

Nivaan Home Loans are available for loan amounts starting from ₹3 Lakh and going up to ₹40 Lakh, subject to the applicant's eligibility, income and applicable lending criteria.

Nivaan Home Loans offer flexible repayment tenure of up to 20 years, subject to the applicable product terms and eligibility criteria.

Applicants generally need proof of identity and address such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID or Driving Licence. Income proof, bank statements for the last 12 months and details of existing loans may also be required.

Nivaan Home Loans offer floating interest rate options. The applicable interest rate depends on the loan product, applicant profile and prevailing terms.

Yes. Nivaan provides balance transfer options for eligible customers who want to transfer their existing home loan. The transfer is subject to applicable eligibility, documentation and loan terms.

Part-payment and prepayment options are available for eligible Home Loan customers, subject to the applicable terms, conditions and charges.
